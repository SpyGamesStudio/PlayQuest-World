## 👥 PlayQuest Characters

The main cast of **PlayQuest**. Henry, James, Kayla, Ava, Zoey and Abigail

---

## Henry Schwartz

- **Age:** 19
- **Birthday:** September 2002
- **Role:** Main Protagonist

A kind-hearted adventurer who always tries to help others. Henry is the central protagonist of PlayQuest and often finds himself leading the group through new adventures.

---

## James Walton

- **Age:** 19
- **Birthday:** December 2002
- **Former Name:** Leon

A dependable member of the team who often balances serious moments with humor.

---

## Michael Schmidt

- **Age:** 19
- **Birthday:** November 2002
- **Status:** Deceased
- **Cause of Death:** Entity Override

Although no longer alive during the events of PlayQuest, Michael's story remains an important part of the game's lore.

---

## Kayla Sawyer

- **Age:** 18
- **Birthday:** January 2003

A gentle and caring member of the group. Kayla is Henry's partner and is often teased by her friends whenever she gets flustered around him.

---

## Ava Carter

- **Age:** 18
- **Birthday:** March 2003
- **Former Name:** Rose

A calm and thoughtful friend who brings a peaceful perspective to the group's adventures.

---

## Zoey Harper

- **Age:** 18
- **Birthday:** August 2003
- **Former Name:** Giselle

An energetic and playful member of the group who enjoys spending time with her friends and bringing excitement to every adventure.

---

## Abigail Grace

- **Age:** 18
- **Birthday:** April 2003

A close friend of Kayla and Zoey. Abigail is observant and quick to notice the little things happening around the group.
